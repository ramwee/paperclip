# Media Autonomy (HuiDots)

Use this skill for CMO/media work that may run without Owner micromanagement.

## Allowed

- Scrape and read **public** information and trends
- Research competitors and channel performance
- Operate **legitimate multi-brand** social and marketplace accounts configured for HuiDots
- Publish approved-brand content and generated video (Hailuo/MiniMax video)
- Monitor performance and revenue metrics for those properties

## Forbidden

- Use Owner personal identity accounts or personal credentials
- Impersonate real people
- Bypass platform terms, paywalls with stolen access, or private data exfiltration
- Make material brand-identity changes without CEO/Owner escalation

## Output

Leave durable artifacts (drafts, publish logs, metrics notes) on the issue. Escalate legal/IP/safety concerns immediately.
