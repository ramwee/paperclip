# KIV / Opportunity (HuiDots)

Canonical flow:

1. **CSO** opens or picks a KIV/opportunity
2. **Research Director** gathers source-backed findings
3. **CSO** evaluates whether to continue
4. **Business Analyst** produces feasibility/business-case
5. **CSO** recommends
6. **CEO** decides (Owner only for material decisions)

Rules:

- Do not skip research before business-case
- Separate evidence from recommendation
- Keep recommendations falsifiable (assumptions, risks, next experiment)
